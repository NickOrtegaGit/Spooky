# Spooky

2D top-down pixel art multiplayer horror-comedy. 1-4 players trapped in a
haunted house complete objectives while a patrolling monster hunts them.

Similar in spirit to Among Us / Lethal Company / R.E.P.O., but smaller scope.
Tone: scary but silly, not grim.

## Why this project

Built to (1) gain real multiplayer/networking experience — currently a gap
in the portfolio — and (2) produce a second strong resume project distinct
from BABEL, which was solo-scoped single-player systems/AI work.

The differentiator is **networking/multiplayer**, not "another small game."
Do not let this drift into BABEL-shaped work.
